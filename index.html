HOW IT WORKS

Simple. Secure. Transparent.

From reporting an incident to receiving updates, BalangayLog helps keep residents informed.

01

Submit a Report

Provide the incident details and attach supporting evidence when available.

Authorized barangay personnel review reports and update their progress.
